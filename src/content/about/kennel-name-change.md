---

We recently changed our kennel name from Parsons Shepherds to **Moro Shepherds**. The name "Moro" reflects our deep connection to the mountains and the heritage of the White Swiss Shepherd breed.

Our commitment to producing healthy, well-tempered White Swiss Shepherds remains unchanged. All dogs registered under the Parsons Shepherds name are the same dogs you know and love — only the kennel name has changed.

If you have any questions about the name change or need updated documentation for a dog you purchased from us, please don't hesitate to [contact us](/contact).
