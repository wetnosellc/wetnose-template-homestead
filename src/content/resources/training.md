White Swiss Shepherds are highly intelligent and eager to please, which makes them a joy to train — but it also means they need consistent, positive guidance from an early age.

## Starting Early

Begin training as soon as your puppy comes home. Simple commands like "sit," "stay," and "come" can be introduced at 8 weeks. Use short sessions (5–10 minutes) to match your puppy's attention span.

## Positive Reinforcement

This breed responds best to reward-based training. Use treats, praise, and play as rewards. Avoid harsh corrections — White Swiss Shepherds can be sensitive and harsh methods can damage their trust and confidence.

## Socialization

Expose your puppy to different people, places, sounds, and animals during the critical socialization window (8–16 weeks). Puppy classes are a great way to combine socialization with basic training.

## Beyond the Basics

Once your dog has mastered basic obedience, consider:

- **AKC Canine Good Citizen (CGC)** — a great foundation test
- **Obedience trials** — this breed excels at competition obedience
- **Agility** — White Swiss Shepherds love agility courses
- **Tracking and nose work** — engage their natural herding instincts
- **Therapy dog certification** — their gentle temperament makes them excellent therapy dogs
