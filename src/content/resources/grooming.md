The White Swiss Shepherd's beautiful white double coat requires regular maintenance, but is not as demanding as it might look. With a consistent routine, grooming is manageable and even enjoyable for both you and your dog.

## Brushing

Brush your dog **2–3 times per week** to prevent mats and reduce shedding. During shedding seasons (spring and fall), daily brushing is recommended. Use a slicker brush to work through the outer coat and an undercoat rake to remove the dense undercoat.

## Bathing

Bathe every **4–6 weeks**, or as needed. Use a whitening shampoo designed for white-coated dogs to keep the coat bright. Always blow-dry fully after bathing — a damp undercoat can cause skin irritation.

## Shedding

White Swiss Shepherds are moderate-to-heavy shedders and "blow" their undercoat twice a year. A deshedding tool (like a Furminator) used a few times during shedding season can dramatically reduce loose hair.

## Nails

Trim nails every **3–4 weeks**. If you can hear clicking on hard floors, it's time for a trim. Introduce nail trimming early so your puppy learns to accept it calmly.

## Ears

Check ears weekly for redness, odor, or discharge. Clean with a vet-approved ear cleaner as needed. White Swiss Shepherds with erect ears have good air circulation and are less prone to ear infections than floppy-eared breeds.

## Teeth

Brush teeth **2–3 times per week** with dog-safe toothpaste. Dental chews and water additives can supplement brushing.

## Professional Grooming

A professional groomer every 2–3 months can handle trimming around the paws, sanitary areas, and ears, and provides a thorough bath and blow-out.
